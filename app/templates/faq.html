Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We probably have answers. If not, get in touch.

1. How accurate is the BleedRate calculator? ▼

BleedRate provides estimates based on official South African tax rates from SARS and Treasury. However, tax calculations are complex and your actual tax liability may differ based on:

  • Your specific circumstances (age, dependents, medical status)
  • Personal deductions and exemptions you qualify for
  • Provincial variations in rates and municipal charges
  • Your unique income structure (salary vs. business vs. investments)
  • Tax law changes mid-year

Remember: This calculator is for educational purposes only and is NOT a substitute for professional tax advice. Always consult a qualified tax professional (CPA, accountant, or tax advisor) before making important tax decisions or filing your taxes.

2. Do you store my data or calculations? ▼

No. Here's what actually happens:

  • Your calculations run in your browser and are NOT transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly save them
  • No permanent storage: When you close your browser, your data is deleted from your device
  • No data selling: We do not sell your personal or financial information to third parties
  • Minimal analytics: We collect basic site analytics (page views, traffic sources) to improve the site, but not your personal data

For complete details, see our Privacy Policy.

3. What taxes does BleedRate include? ▼

BleedRate calculates your TOTAL tax burden, not just income tax. We include:

Direct Taxes

  • PAYE (income tax)
  • UIF contributions
  • Dividends tax
  • Capital gains tax

Consumption Taxes

  • VAT (15%)
  • Fuel levies
  • Excise duties (alcohol, tobacco)
  • Health promotion levy

Property & Transport

  • Municipal rates
  • Vehicle license fees
  • Transfer duty
  • Toll fees

Travel & Other

  • Airport taxes
  • Import duties
  • Municipal utilities
  • Embedded corporate taxes

This gives you the complete picture of how much government revenue you generate annually.

4. Why is my BleedRate number so much higher than my income tax? ▼

Because you're seeing the REAL number for the first time.

Income tax (PAYE) is only about 30-40% of your total tax burden. The rest comes from everywhere else:

  • VAT: 15% of everything you spend
  • Fuel levies: ~R6 per litre, 12 months a year
  • Excise duties: On alcohol, tobacco, vehicles
  • Municipal charges: Water, sewerage, refuse, rates
  • Embedded corporate taxes: Hidden in prices you pay
  • Travel taxes: Airport fees, accommodation levies

Result: You typically pay 30-50% of gross income to government when you add everything up. Most people only see PAYE on their paystub, so they think they're paying less.

5. Is BleedRate a substitute for professional tax advice? ▼

Absolutely NOT.

BleedRate is an educational tool, not professional tax advice. Tax law is complex, and your personal situation affects your actual liability significantly:

  • Deductions: Medical expenses, donations, mortgage interest, business expenses
  • Exemptions: Rebates based on age, status, disability
  • Income structure: Salary vs. business income vs. investments
  • Province: Different municipal and provincial rates
  • Timing: When you earn vs. spend

For accurate filing, tax planning, or major financial decisions, consult a qualified tax professional, CPA, or accountant.

6. How often are tax rates updated? ▼

Tax rates in South Africa change annually. Here's the timeline:

  • February/March: Government announces new rates for the tax year starting April 1st
  • April: New rates take effect for the 2024/25 tax year (or whatever the current year is)
  • Quarterly: Some rates change mid-year (fuel levies, for example)

Our process: We update BleedRate rates as soon as official changes are published. We always show the "Last Updated" date on the calculator.

Check the footer or settings to see the date rates were last refreshed. If it looks outdated, let us know!

7. Why don't you include deductions or exemptions? ▼

BleedRate provides baseline estimates for a standard taxpayer. Deductions and exemptions vary widely by individual:

  • Medical expenses (rebates based on premiums paid)
  • Charitable donations (subject to limits)
  • Mortgage interest (varies by property)
  • Business expenses (only if self-employed)
  • Age-based rebates (60+, 65+)

To adjust your estimate, use the Retirement Contributions field, which reduces your taxable income. But for a complete estimate that includes ALL your deductions, you'll need to work with a tax professional.

8. Can I use BleedRate for previous tax years? ▼

BleedRate is designed for the current tax year only.

Tax rates change every year, so a 2026 calculator won't be accurate for 2025 or 2024. If you need to calculate liability for a previous year:

  • Work with a tax professional who has historical rate tables
  • Contact SARS directly for official calculations
  • Request historical rates from the Treasury

We may add a year-selector feature in the future, but it's not a priority right now.

9. Is BleedRate an official SARS tool? ▼

No. BleedRate is an independent, third-party educational tool.

  • Created by: Louis Pieterse (independent developer)
  • Official endorsement: None. BleedRate is NOT endorsed or maintained by SARS
  • Data source: We use official SARS tax rates and regulations, but the calculator is built independently

For official tax calculations, filing, or disputes with SARS, visit www.sars.gov.za or contact SARS directly.

10. How do I reduce my tax burden? ▼

BleedRate shows you where your money flows. That insight is the first step. Here are some strategies to explore:

  • Maximize retirement savings: Contributions reduce taxable income (tax-free growth)
  • Fuel-efficient vehicles: Lower fuel consumption = lower fuel levies
  • Buy locally-assembled cars: No 25% import duty (vs. imported vehicles)
  • Claim all eligible deductions: Medical expenses, donations, business expenses
  • Optimize investment structure: Tax-free savings accounts, ETFs, etc.
  • Adjust consumption: High-tax items (alcohol, tobacco) have high levies
  • Strategic giving: Donations qualify for tax deductions

But remember: Tax planning is complex and highly personal. Don't guess—work with a qualified tax professional or financial advisor who knows your complete situation. They can create a real strategy that actually works for YOU.
