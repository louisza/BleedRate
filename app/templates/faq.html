Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about BleedRate, taxes, and how to use the calculator.

How accurate are the estimates?

The estimates are based on published SARS rates and official government levies for the 2026/27 tax year. They are educational and indicative, not professional tax advice.

Why do my results differ from my payslip?

Payslips show PAYE and contributions. BleedRate calculates the full government take including VAT, fuel levies, municipal rates and embedded corporate tax—items that don't appear on payslips.

Can I rely on BleedRate for filing taxes?

No. BleedRate is an educational tool. For filing and legally binding advice, consult a registered tax practitioner.

Do you store my inputs?

By default we do not store your inputs server-side. Calculations run in the browser unless you choose to save a scenario.

How do I report a bug or suggest rates changes?

Open an issue on our GitHub: GitHub Issues or email contact@bleedrate.co.za.
