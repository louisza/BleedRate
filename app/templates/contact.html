Contact BleedRate

BleedRate is an independent project built to help South Africans understand their true tax footprint. We welcome questions about the calculator, corrections to tax rates, suggestions for new features, and feedback on content accuracy. Whether you've spotted a bug, noticed an outdated rate, or simply want to tell us the number made you angry, we'd like to hear from you.

📧 Email

The best way to reach us:

contact@bleedrate.co.za

We aim to respond within a few business days. During busy periods it may take up to a week.

🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

For technical issues, incorrect calculations, or feature suggestions, please open a GitHub issue. This is the fastest way to get bugs fixed, as issues are tracked publicly and prioritised in our development backlog.

Open a GitHub Issue →

Please note: BleedRate is maintained by a solo developer in their spare time. We read every message and do our best to respond, but response times are typically a few days. Thank you for your patience and for using BleedRate.
