About BleedRate

"See exactly where your money goes to government"

Our Mission

BleedRate exists for one reason: to show you the complete truth about your tax burden—not just income tax, but the total government revenue hidden in every aspect of your life.

Most tax calculators focus narrowly on income tax. BleedRate goes further. We calculate:

  • Direct taxes: Income tax (PAYE), UIF, dividends tax
  • Consumption taxes: VAT, fuel levies, excise duties on alcohol and tobacco
  • Utility taxes: Electricity levies, health promotion levy, plastic bag levy
  • Property taxes: Municipal rates, transfer duty, vehicle license fees
  • Travel taxes: Airport taxes, fuel levies on every litre of petrol
  • Hidden taxes: Import duties, embedded corporate taxes in prices

The Result: Most people pay 30-50% of their gross income to government—far more than they realize—because they only see their paystub, not the full picture.

Why This Matters

Financial Awareness

You can't manage what you don't measure. Knowing your true tax burden is the first step to intelligent financial planning.

Informed Voting

How do you evaluate a politician's tax policy if you don't know your own tax burden? BleedRate helps you understand what you're actually paying.

Strategic Decisions

Want to reduce your tax burden? First, see where the money flows. Should you live in a different province? Buy a locally-assembled car instead of imported? The calculator shows the impact of real decisions.

Accountability

Government revenue is taxpayer money. Transparency about tax burden creates accountability. If citizens knew the real numbers, policy would shift.

The Problem We Solve

❌ The Old Way

"I earn R50,000/month. My income tax is about R7,000. So I pay 14% tax."

This person is missing R15,000-R20,000/month in hidden government revenue.

✅ The BleedRate Way

"I earn R50,000/month. After ALL taxes (income, VAT, fuel, utilities, property, travel, embedded corporate taxes), I pay about R20,000/month to government. That's 40% of my income."

This person now sees reality and can make informed decisions.

Accuracy & Disclaimers

⚠️ Important Disclaimers

  • Not Professional Tax Advice: This calculator is for educational purposes only. It is NOT a substitute for professional tax advice from a qualified tax professional or accountant. Always consult a professional before making tax-related decisions.
  • Estimates Only: BleedRate provides estimates based on published tax rates. Your actual tax liability may differ based on:
    • Personal circumstances (rebates, deductions, exemptions)
    • Provincial variations
    • Changes in tax law (rates are updated annually)
    • Individual business income or investment structures
  • Data Sources: Tax rates are sourced from SARS official publications, Treasury releases, and tax year 2024/25 legislation. However, tax law is complex and subject to interpretation. We do our best to be accurate, but we cannot guarantee 100% accuracy.

How We Ensure Accuracy

✅ Official Sources

All tax rates are pulled from official SARS publications and Treasury documents.

✅ Annual Updates

Tax rates change yearly. We update BleedRate every tax year (typically March/April) with new rates.

✅ Open Source

BleedRate's code is open source on GitHub. Anyone can review, audit, and suggest improvements.

✅ Community Review

We welcome corrections and feedback. If you find an error, please report it via GitHub or email.

What We DON'T Do

  • ❌ We don't store your data: Your calculations are processed in your browser and not saved to our servers (unless you choose to save them).
  • ❌ We don't sell your information: We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • ❌ We don't provide tax filing services: We can't file your taxes for you. We're a calculator, not an accountant.
  • ❌ We don't provide personalized tax advice: We show estimates for your profile. Real tax planning requires a professional.
  • ❌ We don't track you: We use minimal analytics. We don't follow you across the web.
